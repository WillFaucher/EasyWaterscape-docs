# Using CoastMaker

## Open the CoastMaker Example Level
The example CoastMaker level starts you off with everything already set up correctly so that you can get an idea of what to expect.

To see the effect of CoastMaker, click the water in your viewport, and in the details panel on the right, scroll down until you find the “CoastMaker” tab, expand the dropdown menu there, and click “CoastMaker”.

CoastMaker captures any and all static meshes and landscapes that are found within the “Coverage Size” volume (The big square volume that surrounds the island as shown here)
