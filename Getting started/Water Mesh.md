#Water Mesh & Density

## Mesh Mode

- Go to your library on FAB
- Click on "Add to Project"
- Open the Unreal Engine project you have added EasyWaterscape to.
- You will find the EasyWaterscape folder in your project's content browser.

## 2. Mesh Density

- For starters, please refer to the provided Demo/Example levels found in the "Content/EasyWaterscape/Levels-Examples" folder.
- Here you will find a level for each aspect of EasyWaterscape for you to get started and learn from: CoastMaker, Buoyancy, Underwater, etc.
- In order to see the water simulation, click on the water itself, or, select BP_EasyWaterscape in the outliner. In the details panel on the right hand side, click on "Run EasyWater".
