# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is EasyWaterscape performant enough for games, or is it mostly for Cinematics?
EasyWaterscape is designed with both quality and performance in mind. Of course it depends on your target platform, check out the [Benchmarks Section](<Benchmarks.md>) to gauge the performance. 
The benchmarks were run with Mid settings, meaning there is some leeway to optimize even more if you need to. EasyWaterscape offers many performance options, dials for you to control and fine tune the performance to quality ratio you need for your project.

As of October 2026, EasyWaterscape just recieved a major performance update, making it more usable for games than ever, and you have the options at your disposal to really adjust for your target platform.

## Can EasyWaterscape do rivers?
No. EasyWaterscape is primarily designed for bodies of water, such as Oceans/Seas, Lakes, Ponds, even pools. But rivers/streams involve fluid dynamics
that EasyWaterscape does not support yet. Rivers are currently on the development roadmap, and we intend to attempt them in the near future, but this will involve
a few months of development work and thorough testing for us to decide if the results meet our standards.

## Is EasyWaterscape a complete game solution package?
Not quite. EasyWaterscape is designed with both games and cinematics in mind, with a strong focus on optimization, perfectly suitable for realtime use. 
But it is not meant to be a package complete with gameplay mechanics to plug and play into a nautical game, for example. You will need to implement those features yourself. 
EasyWaterscape is optimized, built cleanly, meaning the blueprint functions are all organised and clearly labeled and should be easy for your team to adapt it to your needs.
